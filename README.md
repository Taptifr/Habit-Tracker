Aura is a personalized habit tracking app designed to adapt to your unique style and personality.
Unlike generic productivity tools, Aura learns who you are through your preferences, goals, and patterns,
creating a tailored experience that feels like your own personal hype universe.

**Features**
>Personalised Aesthetics
>Habit Tracking
- **Daily Tasks**: Add tasks with smart deadlines (15m, 30m, 1h, 2h, eod)
- **Goal Management**: Set long-term goals with milestone tracking
- **Streak Tracking**: Monitor your consistency with visual streak counters
- **Progress Visualization**: See your daily completion percentage
> Ai powered coaching
>  Daily Moments
- **Photo Journaling**: Capture and caption daily photos
- **Mood Tracking**: Reflect on your day with personalized questions
- **Achievement Celebrations**: Confetti and rewards for completed tasks
